Reading as a durable practice

Last updated: August 2026

A personal site becomes useful through accumulation rather than novelty. Its typography should make returning, revising, and reading feel natural without turning the interface into the main subject.

This paragraph includes strong text, genuine emphasis, a descriptive prose link, and inline code such as font-display: swap.

A hierarchy for long-form reading

Headings introduce the material that follows. They stay closer to the section they own than to the previous section, preserving a readable direction even when color and decoration are absent.

Lists retain the prose rhythm
  • Body text remains the calibration point.
  • Supporting information stays compact without becoming disposable.
    • Nested ideas remain visibly subordinate.
    • List density does not collapse the reading rhythm.
  • Components do not invent private type scales.
